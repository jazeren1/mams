# NAS Layout

## Current roots

Fitness, Kids Movies, Kids Shows, Movies, Music, TV.

## Version 1 policy

Do not perform a mass migration. Support flat movies, movie folders, series folders without season folders, and series folders with season folders.

## New content

Movies may remain flat. TV-like media should use series and season folders.

## Temporary backup

Create a root outside Plex libraries:

```text
MAMS-Backup/
  replacements/
  failed/
  quarantine/
```

## Local staging

```text
Media Archive/
  Incoming/
  Needs Review/
```
