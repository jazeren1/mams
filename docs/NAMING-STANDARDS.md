# Naming Standards

## Movies and Kids Movies

```text
Title (Year).mkv
```

Examples: `District 9 (2009).mkv`, `The Thing (1982).mkv`.

Do not include codec, resolution, source, ripper, or checksum in filenames.

## TV, Kids Shows, and Fitness

```text
Series Name/
  Season 01/
    Series Name - S01E01 - Episode Title.mkv
```

Episode title may be omitted. Use two-digit season and episode numbers. Use `S01E01-E02` only when a multi-episode file cannot reasonably be split.

Store source type—DVD, BLURAY, UHD_BLURAY—in the database.
